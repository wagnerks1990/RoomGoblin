How you earn your points

Participation, feedback & late-work rules

Use your own reasoning. Feedback and scores are generated automatically in your teacher's teaching style; your teacher can review, correct, or replace them. No points are awarded for likes, speed, or number of posts.

Your Schoology display name is used on class posts when available; otherwise your assigned discussion name is used. Do not include other personal information in your writing. The configured grading service receives your writing and the selected peer context, without account names or roster details.

Late work earns 50% of earned points, applied once. Acceptance ends at the posted next-class cutoff unless your teacher has preapproved an exception. The class date alone does not set a deadline.
