The board, horse/fortress forms, armor, clothing, accessories and cinematic
choreography were created for RoomGoblin. Carved human faces use a head/neck-only
derivative of the explicitly CC0 MakeHuman hm08 basemesh; see assets/MAKEHUMAN-NOTICE.txt
and assets/MAKEHUMAN-CC0.txt for exact source, hash, authors and modifications.
No downloaded textures, fonts, movie artwork, movie character models, music or
proprietary Wizarding World assets are included.

This optional visual renderer does not determine move legality or game outcomes.
