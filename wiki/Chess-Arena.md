# Chess Arena and teaching

Teachers monitor and play at `/learn/chess`; students launch freshly exported
Chess SCORM packages. Existing assignment capabilities and private participant
bearers authorize play. Schoology-reported names provide attribution without a
separate password; they do not verify identity or establish attendance.

Chess teaching includes six piece guides, five lesson stages and fourteen
independent questions with explanations. Its requested position examples are
restricted server-side to owned active solo/computer practice with a current
revision and available assignment/seat. All multiplayer hints are denied,
including teachers and watchers. A request returns legal examples rather than
best-move rankings and never changes moves, clocks, participation points or
standings. Lessons and in-memory question progress do not award verified mastery.

The local `/chess/leaderboard` TV page remains a separate read-only projection
with first-name/last-initial names. Teacher full-name access remains class-scoped.
Teacher pages/assets and TV routes remain unavailable at the isolated student
gateway. No teaching request authorizes classroom effects or physical attendance.

The optional 3D cast uses a documented CC0 head derivative and original horse, fortress, armor and motion. Other realistic external models remain
research candidates whose archives have not been imported. A source-page license
does not establish that every file in a future download is cleared. Live Schoology
launch/resume/reporting and classroom GPU acceptance remain separate release gates.
